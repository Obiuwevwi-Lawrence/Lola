Background

About Me

My name is Omotomilola Jegede. I am a PhD Candidate in Health Services Research (Public Health) at Old Dominion University, with a multidisciplinary background spanning statistics, computational and applied mathematics, public health, research, and data analytics.

I hold a Master of Science in Computational and Applied Mathematics from Old Dominion University and a Master of Science in Mathematical Sciences (Statistics) from Georgia Southern University. I earned my B.Tech. in Mathematical Sciences with a concentration in Industrial Mathematics from the Federal University of Technology Akure.

My work uses quantitative and qualitative methods to examine public health challenges, including substance misuse and overdose, infectious disease dynamics, health disparities, chronic disease, community health, and program evaluation. I work across the research lifecycle, from survey design and data management to statistical analysis, visualization, interpretation, and dissemination.

Alongside research, I have served in major graduate leadership roles at ODU, including President of the Graduate Students Government Association, while supporting faculty development, student success, and academic-community engagement.
